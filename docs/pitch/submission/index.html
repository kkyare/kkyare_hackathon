Weather Decision Gym

Field-level weather and yield forecasts for US crop insurers, retrained on every season’s real harvest.

Target market

US crop insurers and reinsurers in the Corn Belt (Iowa, Illinois, Minnesota), for corn and soybean policies.

The same verifier is sold to US RL eval and data companies as auto-graded weather tasks.

Problem

73%

of US crop insurance payouts are weather-driven ($118.7B, 2001–2022). Yet pricing and claim checks run on county-level weather. No one has field-level weather-to-yield data checked against real harvests.

Source: EWG analysis of USDA RMA data
