## Read for the SaaS idea

- **Australia is the strongest market.** It has numeric label rules (3–20 km/h wind, no hazardous inversion), state record-keeping law, and record crop area (25.2 Mha).
- **Canada has a weaker rules hook but a fresh trigger.** There is no numeric window, but drone spraying was legalised in June 2026, and Saskatchewan now requires GPS flight records.
- **The US drone-operator segment is the fastest-growing small market:** 1,710 operators, growing about 58% a year.
